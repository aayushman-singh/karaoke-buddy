What it does

Three moves that turn a YouTube-style karaoke clip into something you can actually sing along to.

Pitch shift

Nudge the whole song up or down by semitones so the melody lands in your comfortable range — tempo stays untouched.

Vocal reduction

Pull down a leftover guide vocal so the lead voice steps back and the room is yours to fill.

One-click export

Render the adjusted track to MP4 video or MP3/M4A audio — ready for the party, the practice room, or the road.

Two ways to run it

Same idea, two engines. Grab the desktop app for the full quality, or poke at the demo without installing a thing.

Desktop The full engine

The Windows app does the real work: studio-grade pitch shifting powered by Rubber Band, with bundled FFmpeg and mpv for clean playback and export. This is the one to install.

Browser A quick preview

The web demo is a lightweight browser preview built on SoundTouch / WSOLA. It is great for a fast taste of the controls — the desktop app is where the quality lives.
